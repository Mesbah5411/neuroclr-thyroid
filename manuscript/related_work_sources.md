# Related Work source audit

This is a focused narrative review, not a systematic review or an exhaustive novelty search. Studies were included for tabular thyroid classification, feature selection, stacking, or methodological relevance, not their performance relative to NeuroCLR. The table consistently omits cross-study accuracy ranking, including NeuroCLR's score.

- Akhtar 2021: supplied electronics-10-03026.pdf, Sections 2.1, 2.2, and 3; hospital cohort 309, three classes, 75:25 split; paper also mentions a second dataset, so table scope explicitly restricted to documented hospital experiment. DOI 10.3390/electronics10233026.
- Ji 2024: supplied pone.0295501.pdf, Sections 3/3.1, Table 4; five selected codes, 230 each, 80:20 split; also cross-validation. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0295501.
- Rehman 2021: publisher abstract and metadata verified at https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13369-020-05206-x . Full text subscription-limited; no unsupported exact split or cohort count included.
- Chaganti 2022: publisher PDF https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/cancers/cancers-14-03914/article_deploy/cancers-14-03914.pdf . Abstract and Introduction support selection strategies and RF finding. Simple Summary says ten diseases whereas Introduction says five; avoid copying that inconsistent count and avoid secondary-source sample count 1774.
- Hassan 2025: publisher full text https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/20552076251341430 . Methods and dataset sections support 3772 records, RFE, SMOTE, 80:20 and ten-fold validation; LR meta-learner verified. Abstract and body differ in base-learner count, so no exact ensemble size specified.
- Methodological sources: existing verified Wolpert, Aitchison, Huang, Carriero, Kapoor, and Hendrickx bibliography entries.
- Source dates/publishers are not disguised: relevant 2021/2022 domain precedents and non-Elsevier journals are deliberately included because they are directly relevant to the supplied papers.

No external validation, prospective deferral validation, universal novelty, or cross-study superiority is claimed.

## Recent Elsevier studies: source-to-claim check

Publication years follow journal issue records. DOI years may differ. Specific findings were checked against the papers or the linked author versions.
- xie2025thyroid — [Thyroid disease diagnosis based on feature interpolation Interaction and dynamic assignment Stacking model](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bspc.2024.107207). Author manuscript, pp. 6--11: interpolation of missing predictors and dynamic assignment in the second stacking stage on UCI thyroid data. [Text checked](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-2851005/v1/6037550e-db5b-4ac3-853c-4ed38a3754c5.pdf?c=1682633383).
- kayahan2026thyroid — [From objective grouping to fuzzy reference intervals: A standardized machine learning approach for thyroid function tests](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cca.2025.120635). Methods: fuzzy clustering and thyroid function test reference intervals. The citation is about interval estimation, not disease classification. [Text checked](https://avesis.atauni.edu.tr/publication/details/49cedbf0-c83f-4e35-96c2-1a3eca0ed745/oai/document.pdf).
- kablan2023stacking — [Evaluation of stacked ensemble model performance to predict clinical outcomes: A COVID-19 study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2023.105090). Methods and Results: hospital COVID-19 outcome study varied feature subsets, base learners and meta-learners. [Text checked](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10165871/).
- wu2026tabular — [Systematic evaluation of machine learning models for clinical risk prediction on real-world hospital datasets](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2026.115654). Summary and Methods: ten models across eight real-world clinical risk datasets, assessing discrimination, calibration and clinical utility. [Text checked](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13127387/).
- gehringer2024multinomial — [How to develop, validate, and update clinical prediction models using multinomial logistic regression](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2024.111481). Model evaluation section: development, external validation, and updating of multinomial clinical prediction models. [Text checked](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.12008).
- brown2026calibration — [Community medical centers struggle to produce well-calibrated clinical prediction models: Data augmentation can help](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2026.105012). Results: smaller sites had 3,187 and 905 records available; estimated requirements for recalibration were 16,461 and 15,299, respectively. Scope is limited to those sites and their specific requirements. [Text checked](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13629187/).
- tan2025structured — [Integrated analysis for electronic health records with structured and sporadic missingness](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2025.104933). Methods: integrated EHR analysis at three Duke hospitals, with structured and sporadic missing values. Distinct from the 2023 Tan paper. [Text checked](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.09208).
- curnow2023imputation — [Multiple imputation of missing data under missing at random: compatible imputation models are not sufficient to avoid bias if they are mis-specified](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2023.06.011). Simulation/scenario evaluation: under missing at random, misspecified imputation or analysis models can yield biased estimates. [Text checked](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7615471/).
- peluso2024uncertainty — [Deep learning uncertainty quantification for clinical text classification](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2023.104576). Clinical text classification: assessed uncertainty-based selective prediction and the tradeoff between retained cases and performance. [Text checked](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/165790/cdc_165790_DS1.pdf).
- hallberg2025reject — [Classification with reject option: Distribution-free error guarantees via conformal prediction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mlwa.2025.100664). Theory and experiments: binary classification, accepting singleton conformal prediction sets, with guarantees under specified conditions. No guarantee transferred to NeuroCLR. [Text checked](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.21802).
- Matos (DOI 10.1016/j.landig.2026.101001) was verified as a fairness-metrics scoping review and removed because the study does not evaluate demographic fairness.
